## Platform

## Product purpose

Thomas Scheiber's personal portfolio presents his professional contributions, personal software projects, websites, qualifications and CV. The work section should let visitors understand what the applications do through useful, interactive demonstrations.

## Confirmed constraints

- Preserve the Mineral light theme, Slate dark theme and System preference selector.
- Keep the CV, credentials, project destinations and contact links working.
- Work demonstrations use fictional data and run entirely in the visitor's browser. They never call work systems, send messages, grant access or create shipments.
- Summaries distinguish Thomas's contributions from the product's overall capabilities. Repository activity is evidence of contribution, not proof of sole ownership, adoption or business impact.
- Research materials stay outside this repository. Public content must omit company branding, internal URLs, staff data, credentials and private source code.

## Work-section direction

The user explicitly prioritized improving the demos and adding meaningful projects found in GitLab. Show decisions, failures and recovery paths instead of static dashboard screenshots. Preserve the established visual system.

## Open decisions

The primary visitor segment has not been specified. The page should be understandable to visitors evaluating engineering work without requiring knowledge of the employer's systems.
