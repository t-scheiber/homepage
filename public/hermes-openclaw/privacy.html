Privacy policy

Updated 21 September 2026

Thomas Scheiber operates Hermes and OpenClaw as a private personal-assistant integration. This policy covers its Google connection and morning briefing.

Information accessed and its purpose

Google sign-in identifies the connected account by its email address. Calendar access lets the assistants read calendar names and event information, including titles, dates, times, locations and participation status, to answer scheduling questions and prepare briefings. Gmail access, when separately granted, supports the email actions Thomas requests. Each Google connection is limited by its granted permissions.

The morning collector currently uses read-only Calendar access. It combines appointments with task names, due dates, priorities and checklist items from the connected TickTick lists, plus weather for the location supplied by Home Assistant.

Processing and sharing

The integration runs on Thomas's own systems. The morning collector creates its calendar and task readout without sending those items to a language model. It sends the briefing text to Home Assistant and Amazon Alexa for speech. Anyone within hearing distance of the speaker may hear it.

Other assistant requests may send the information needed to fulfil the request to the configured AI or delivery provider. Connected providers process that information under their own policies. Google data is not sold or used for advertising. Transfers are limited to providing the requested assistant features, with the account owner's authorisation.

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored separately from source code on Thomas's systems. The morning collector stores its latest briefing and source-availability status locally; a new run replaces those files. Home Assistant also receives the current briefing. Assistant history, service logs and backups can retain earlier information until removed through their respective storage and backup controls. Revoking Google access does not automatically erase those copies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Thomas can also remove local credentials, generated briefings, related history and retained backups from his systems. Contact the address below for questions or requests about retained data.

This information website

These app-information pages do not contain Google account data, sign-in forms, analytics scripts or advertising. The hosting and network providers may process normal web-request information to deliver and protect the pages.
