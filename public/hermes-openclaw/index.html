Hermes and OpenClaw

Private assistants that help Thomas organise his day using the accounts and services he chooses to connect.

Google access

The integration connects Google Calendar and, when separately authorised, Gmail to Thomas's personal assistants. Access depends on the permissions granted on Google's consent screen. The integration is for Thomas's own use and has no public registration.

The morning briefing

The morning automation reads calendar appointments, including university lectures, alongside a weather forecast and TickTick tasks. It sends the resulting briefing to Home Assistant for spoken playback through Amazon Alexa.

The morning collector uses read-only Calendar access. It reads accessible calendar names and event details to announce appointments, times, locations and scheduling conflicts. It does not create, change or delete Google Calendar events.

Your data

The privacy policy explains what information is used, where briefing data goes, and how access can be revoked.
